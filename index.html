DATA PLATFORM MODERNIZATION

Modernizing a high-volume platform for scale

I owned the processing-path redesign, migration strategy, and production validation for a business-critical data platform that had become expensive to operate and difficult to scale. I partnered with release, customer-readiness, and dependent engineering teams to complete the rollout and operational handoff.

I replaced individual-event processing with a summarized-event model and managed risk through parallel validation, readiness gates, telemetry-based verification, and reversible rollout controls. The completed production migration reduced measured downstream event flow by 98% between the legacy and replacement paths. I also corrected a customer-impact analysis gap before the final rollout.

RELIABILITY & AUTOMATION

Lead cross-layer production investigations

I led a cross-layer investigation that proved a production failure was operating-system specific rather than environmental. Working with a platform team, we isolated the defect and carried it through correction and validation handoff.

I later eliminated a recurring class of false-positive processing alerts and built diagnostic tools for health checks, degradation detection, and contextual log analysis. The work removed avoidable operational noise and helped engineers investigate production issues more independently.

SECURE CLOUD ENGINEERING

Design safer release controls

I designed a release safeguard that removes temporary privileged access after failed or canceled deployments, rejecting a broad cleanup approach that could interfere with unrelated stages.

I coordinated dependencies across five engineering teams during a regulated-cloud infrastructure rollout. The teams completed the rollout using a reusable deployment playbook that made subsequent onboarding more consistent.
